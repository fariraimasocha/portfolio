# DomainFlex: The Arcade for SEO Enthusiasts

[DomainFlex](https://domainflex.farirai.workers.dev/) turns checking your Ahrefs Domain Rating into a game. Punch in a URL, get your DR instantly, claim a badge tier, and take your spot on the global leaderboard — no signup, no Ahrefs account, no payment.

It is built for anyone who cares about SEO and wants to see where their domain stands without logging into a paid tool first.

## The Problem

Domain Rating is one of the most-cited SEO numbers, but checking it usually means an Ahrefs account and a paywall. For a quick "how strong is this domain?" check, that flow is too heavy — and there is nothing fun about it.

DomainFlex removes the friction. One URL gets you a score, a badge, and a ranking, instantly and for free.

## How It Works

1. **Enter a domain** — Paste any URL. No account required to play.
2. **Get your DR** — Receive an instant Domain Rating powered by Ahrefs data.
3. **Unlock a badge** — Your score maps to a five-tier badge, from Rookie to Elite.
4. **Climb the leaderboard** — See how your domain ranks against everyone else's, globally.
5. **Embed your badge** — Drop an auto-updating DR badge on your own site.

## Key Features

### Free DR Checker

Look up any domain's Ahrefs Domain Rating in seconds. No signup, no Ahrefs subscription, no payment — just a URL and a score.

### Global Leaderboard

Every checked domain can claim a spot on a worldwide ranking. It turns a static metric into an ongoing competition where higher DR means a better position.

### Five-Tier Badge System

Scores unlock badges from Rookie up to Elite. The tiers give a quick, shareable read on how strong a domain is at a glance.

### Embeddable, Auto-Updating Badges

Grab a badge for your own website that refreshes as your DR changes — proof of your ranking that stays current without manual updates.

### Shareable Ranking Cards

Generated cards make it easy to flex your DR on social or drop it into a portfolio.

DomainFlex is 100% free and requires no signup to play.
