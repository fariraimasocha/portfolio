# VercelLens: Vercel Analytics on Android

[VercelLens](https://vercellens.fariraimasocha.co.zw/) is an Android app that puts Vercel website analytics in your pocket. Connect your Vercel account, see all your sites in one list, and pull up live traffic data for any of them without opening a browser.

It is built for developers who want to check on a deployment, monitor a launch, or glance at weekend traffic without sitting down at a laptop.

## The Problem

Vercel's analytics dashboard is browser-only. Checking traffic means opening a laptop, navigating to the dashboard, and finding the right project. For a quick status check between meetings or while away from the desk, that flow is too heavy.

VercelLens keeps that loop short. One tap opens any site's analytics. The data is live.

## How It Works

1. **Connect your account** — Authenticate with your Vercel account. No separate login required.
2. **Browse your projects** — Every Vercel-hosted site appears in a searchable list with its live URL.
3. **Open analytics** — Tap any site to see real-time visitor counts, page views, and bounce rate.
4. **Adjust the range** — Switch between 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days to see trends over time.

## Key Features

### Live Traffic Monitoring

Visitor counts and page views update in real time. Bounce rate trends show whether recent changes are moving the needle. The data reflects what is happening on the site right now, not a cached snapshot from hours ago.

### Multi-Site Management

All Vercel projects appear in one consolidated list. Search narrows the list quickly when you have many sites. Each entry shows the project name and its live URL, so the right site is always one tap away.

### Flexible Time Ranges

Analytics views span 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. The shorter window is useful for watching a fresh deployment. The longer window is better for spotting weekly patterns or measuring the tail of a launch.

### Per-Page Breakdowns

Traffic breakdowns show which pages are driving the most engagement. That makes it easy to see whether a new post is getting traction or which route is pulling the most visitors on a given day.
