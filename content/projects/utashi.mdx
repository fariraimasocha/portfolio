# Utashi Health: Dermatology, Finally Written for Skin of Color

[Utashi Health](https://utashihealth.com/) is a dermatology platform for people with melanin-rich skin, delivered entirely over WhatsApp. Describe a skin concern, optionally share a photo, and get guidance reviewed by a trained team supervised by practising clinicians — plus a library of condition guides written for your skin, not adapted for it.

It is built for anyone who has read a dermatology article, seen only lighter skin in the photos, and had to guess whether any of it applied to them.

## The Problem

Most dermatology resources are written and illustrated for lighter skin. Conditions that present differently on darker skin — keloids, melasma, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation — are underrepresented or described in ways that do not match what people actually see in the mirror.

The alternative is a clinic: a portal, a form, a wait. For a question like "is this normal, and what do I do about it?", that flow is too heavy.

Utashi keeps it to a WhatsApp message.

## How It Works

1. **Send a message** — Describe your skin concern on WhatsApp. Photos are optional and stay private.
2. **Get an expert review** — A trained team, directly supervised by practising clinicians who understand melanin-rich skin, looks at what you sent.
3. **Receive guidance** — You get tailored condition guides, next steps, and the context you need to manage your care confidently.

## Key Features

### Clinician-Supervised Guidance

Concerns are reviewed by a trained team working under practising clinicians who specialise in melanin-rich skin. It is not an automated symptom checker and not a search result — a person who knows the subject reads what you wrote.

### A Condition Library Built for Darker Skin

Over 40 evidence-based guides cover the conditions that show up most often on melanin-rich skin: keloids, melasma, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, xerosis, and more. Each one is written from the start for skin of color rather than adapted after the fact.

### Reachable on WhatsApp, 24/7

No app to install, no account to create, no portal to log into. Messages can be sent any time; replies typically arrive within a day.

### Education That Assumes You

Articles and guides are written to be acted on — what a condition is, why it happens on your skin, and what genuinely helps — so self-management is informed rather than guesswork.

Utashi Health is clinician-supervised and available to anyone with WhatsApp.
